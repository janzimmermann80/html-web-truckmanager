TruckManager features

A complete tool for carriers and forwarders — from order to invoicing.

🗺️

Dynamic dispatcher board

Organize your drivers' work with a perfect overview of task completion, completed loadings and unloadings and kilometres driven.

📦

Overview of received orders

All jobs colour-coded by execution status. All information on one line, including transport documents.

📅

Loading and unloading diary

Today's planned loadings and unloadings with arrival times and vehicle distances.

⚡

Order intake

Very fast order intake including preparation of instructions for drivers.

🧾

Automatic invoicing from GPS

Based on real transport values (weight, km), the system issues invoices automatically, even in bulk, and sends them by e-mail with transport documents.

📷

Document scanning in the vehicle

Drivers scan transport documents directly in the vehicle and send them to the server for the given transport.

📊

Vehicle economic results

Detailed evaluation of revenue and all costs — fuel consumption and profit charts.

📈

Reports and statistics

The system creates driver and accounting reports by itself and automatically sends them by e-mail on the set day.

🔧

Vehicle service and maintenance

Service planning and notifications. Easy and clear fleet management.

📦

Groupage transport

Each transport is split into collection and distribution. Delivery optimization, warehouse consignment records, and external carriers too.

📱

Driver app

Precise schedule of loadings and unloadings in the phone. Everything is controlled by just 4 buttons.

🔄

Pallet management

Records of returnable packaging during loading and unloading, scanning of pallet notes. Every pallet will be traced.

💳

Driver card backup

Each driver performs regular card backup by themselves using a reader and the app.

💰

Travel allowance calculation

Automatic calculation of per diems and driver travel allowances based on real trips.

🤖

AI assistance

Artificial intelligence helps with planning, evaluation and routine tasks.
